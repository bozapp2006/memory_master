Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

At the start of each level all cards are shown for a few seconds. Memorize them, then flip two cards at a time to find matching pairs. Clear the board to finish the level and earn up to 3 stars.


How do the levels get harder?

Each theme has 100 levels (Beginners has 40). Boards grow from 3x3 to 4x4 and then 5x4. In later levels you'll also meet timers, mistake limits, and twists like mirrored boards, rotating boards, night mode and hardcore levels.


How do I change the theme?

Tap Themes on the home screen, or the palette icon on the level map, and pick any theme. Each theme keeps its own level progress.


What do the power-ups do?

Shuffle reshuffles unmatched cards, Freeze Time pauses the timer, Reveal Pair shows one matching pair, Extra Time adds seconds, Slow Motion slows the timer, and Double Coins / Double XP double your rewards for a level.


Is my progress saved? Will it transfer to a new phone?

Your progress is saved automatically on your device. Because the game has no account, progress is not synced between devices. Deleting the app also deletes its saved progress.


How do I start over?

Open Settings (gear icon on the home screen) and tap Reset Progress. This cannot be undone.


How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Open Settings and use the Sound Effects, Music and Vibration switches. You can also turn on Reduce Motion for fewer animations.


Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Memory Master can be played fully offline, with no ads and no sign-up.


Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how the Memory Master app ("the app") handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, achievements, statistics) and settings are stored only on your device. This data never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you delete the app or use Reset Progress.

Fonts

To display its text, the app may download the Poppins font from Google Fonts the first time it runs with an internet connection. As with any download, Google receives your device's IP address for this request. No other information is sent. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect any data from children.
